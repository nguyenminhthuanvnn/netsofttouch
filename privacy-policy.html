Privacy Policy for Float Video Player

Effective Date: January 01, 2026
Last Updated: August 19, 2026

NetSoftTouch ("we", "us", or "our") built the Float Video Player application as an ad-supported Free application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information and device data when using our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Float Video Player does not require user registration or personal account creation. We do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or physical address) on our servers.

A. Data Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

To display ads, provide crash reporting, and monitor application performance, our app integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect technical and pseudonymous information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, system language.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AdID).
  • Network & Location Data: Coarse IP-based geolocation, network connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G).
  • Usage & Diagnostics Data: App launch frequency, crash logs, session duration, and feature interactions.

B. Third-Party Service Providers

Below are the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used in the app:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

2. Device Permissions Used

Float Video Player requests device permissions solely to provide its core media playback features:

  • Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly to scan, read, and play local video/audio files stored on your device. We do NOT upload your media files to any server.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to display floating video player windows on top of other running apps.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required to keep audio and floating video playback running continuously in the background.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display media playback controls (play, pause, next, previous) in your notification shade.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by advertising partners to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads based on your consent.

3. Advertising and User Consent (GDPR & US State Laws)

  • EEA/UK Users (GDPR / ePrivacy): In compliance with Google's EU User Consent Policy, we utilize Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management Platform (CMP). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK are prompted upon app launch to grant, manage, or revoke consent for personalized ads and cookies/mobile identifiers. You can change your consent preferences anytime via app Settings.
  • California & US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell personal data. Device identifiers used for advertising purposes are processed in accordance with applicable state regulations.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Float Video Player is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete such data.

5. Data Retention, Security, and Deletion Requests

  • Data Retention: Diagnostic and analytics data is retained in aggregated or pseudonymized format according to the data retention policies of Google Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Security: We use standard industry practices to safeguard your information.
  • User Data Deletion: Because our app does not create user accounts, we do not hold personal account databases. You can clear all locally stored preferences and playlists by uninstalling the application or clearing App Data via Android Settings. If you have any inquiries regarding data deletion or third-party identifier removal, please contact us at: netsofttouch@gmail.com.
