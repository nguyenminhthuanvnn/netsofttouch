Privacy Policy for Float Video Player

This application, Float Video Player, is developed by Nguyen Minh Thuan (NetSoftTouch) as an independent personal project. This Service is provided at no cost as an ad-supported application and is intended for use "AS IS".

This Privacy Policy informs users regarding policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information and device data when using this Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Float Video Player does not require user registration or account creation. The developer does not directly collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or address).

A. Data Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

To display advertisements, provide crash reporting, and monitor application performance, the app integrates standard third-party SDKs that may collect technical and pseudonymous information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AdID).
  • Network & Location Data: Coarse IP-based location, network connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G).
  • Usage & Diagnostics Data: App launch frequency, crash logs, session duration, and feature interactions.

B. Third-Party Service Providers

Please review the privacy policies of the third-party service providers utilized in the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
  • AppLovin

2. Device Permissions Used

Float Video Player requests device permissions solely to provide its core media player features:

  • Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly to scan, read, and play local video and audio files stored on your device. Your media files remain on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to display floating video player windows on top of other running applications.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required to keep audio and floating video playback running continuously in the background.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display media playback controls (play, pause, next, previous) in your notification shade.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by advertising partners to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads based on your consent.

3. Advertising and User Consent (GDPR & US State Laws)

  • EEA/UK Users (GDPR / ePrivacy): In compliance with Google's EU User Consent Policy, we utilize Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management Platform (CMP). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK are prompted upon app launch to grant, manage, or revoke consent for personalized ads. You can change your consent preferences anytime via the app Settings.
  • California & US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA): No personal data is sold. Device identifiers used for advertising purposes are processed in accordance with applicable state regulations.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Float Video Player is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention, Security, and Deletion Requests

  • Data Retention: Diagnostic and analytics data is retained in aggregated or pseudonymized format according to the data retention policies of Google Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • User Data Deletion: Because the app does not create user accounts, there is no personal account database. You can clear all locally stored preferences and playlists by uninstalling the application or clearing App Data in Android Settings. If you have any inquiries regarding data deletion or third-party identifier removal, please contact: netsofttouch@gmail.com.
