2.3 Information Processed On-Device Only (Never Collected or Transmitted)

The following data is processed exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to us, Google, or any third party:

Data Type Purpose Permission Used
Media files (Video & Audio) Locally convert, extract, compress, trim, merge, or adjust speed of files you select. READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or system photo/media picker
Contacts Assign a converted audio track as a specific contact's ringtone when requested. A prominent in-app disclosure is displayed before permission is requested. READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS
System Settings Set a converted audio track as the default phone ringtone, notification sound, or alarm. WRITE_SETTINGS
Daily conversion count Enforce the free tier’s daily conversion allowance and any rewarded-ad refills. The counter itself lives in the App’s own private storage, is tied to no account, and resets each day. It is never used to identify you. All video and audio processing is performed 100% on-device by local native FFmpeg libraries. No user files, filenames, or folder paths are ever uploaded to any cloud server.

3. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App integrates Firebase Crashlytics (including native NDK crash handling) to capture application crashes and system errors. Diagnostic reports include stack traces, device state at the time of crash, and high-level non-identifying build keys (e.g. build type, app version, subscription tier). Any error strings originating from conversion tasks are passed through a strict redaction filter before reporting to strip all personal file names, paths, and URIs.

Crash reporting is not switched off by declining ad consent. It carries no advertising identifier and is used only to keep the App working; the crashes that matter most happen in the first seconds of a cold start, before a consent dialog can resolve. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in the security and stability of the App as the legal basis for this processing. You can object to it at any time using the contact address in section 12.

4. Product Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand aggregate usage trends, such as conversion completion rates, screen views, and ad impression revenue. Analytics collection is gated on your consent decision: where a consent dialog is required and you decline, analytics collection is switched off entirely, and where you accept, your finer-grained choices are forwarded to Google as Consent Mode signals (ad storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage). You can change the decision at any time from Settings → Privacy Options (Ad Consent). Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for analytics and advertising is your consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

5. Advertising (Google AdMob & Consent Management)

The free version of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID and device information in accordance with your consent preferences.

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App presents a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (User Messaging Platform / UMP) dialog before any ads are requested or loaded. You can change your consent preferences at any time by navigating to Settings → Privacy Options (Ad Consent).

Ad formats used are banner, interstitial, native, app open, and rewarded. A rewarded ad is only ever shown when you choose to watch one — the App offers it as an option for extra daily conversions, states the reward before the ad plays, and grants that reward only once Google’s ad SDK confirms the ad was watched. Declining costs you nothing and no conversion is deducted.

Subscribers to Monthly Access or Lifetime Pro enjoy an ad-free experience with ad requests and ad-related measurement disabled, and are never offered a rewarded ad.
