2. Information We Collect and Process

2.1 Information You Provide

If you contact us for customer support, we receive the email address and message content you send. We use this solely to respond to your inquiry and assist with your request.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically via integrated Google service SDKs to ensure stability, measure non-identifying performance metrics, serve advertisements, and manage in-app subscriptions:

Data Type Purpose Shared / Processed With
Crash logs & Diagnostics Monitor app stability, analyze fatal crashes, and diagnose conversion failures. Error messages are strictly redacted so no personal filenames or paths are included. Google (Firebase Crashlytics)
App interactions & Analytics Measure feature usage, conversion funnel completions/failures, screen navigation, and aggregate performance. Google (Firebase Analytics)
Advertising ID & Ad Metrics Serve and measure personalized or non-personalized ads (free tier only, subject to user consent). Google (AdMob)
Device & OS Information Crash diagnostic context (device model, OS version, architecture) and ad delivery. Google (Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob)
Purchase & Subscription Tokens Verify and manage subscription / Lifetime Pro entitlement. Google (Google Play Billing)

2.3 Information Processed On-Device Only (Never Collected or Transmitted)

The following data is processed exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to us, Google, or any third party:

Data Type Purpose Permission Used
Media files (Video & Audio) Locally convert, extract, compress, trim, merge, or adjust speed of files you select. READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or system photo/media picker
Contacts Assign a converted audio track as a specific contact's ringtone when requested. A prominent in-app disclosure is displayed before permission is requested. READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS
System Settings Set a converted audio track as the default phone ringtone, notification sound, or alarm. WRITE_SETTINGS

All video and audio processing is performed 100% on-device by local native FFmpeg libraries. No user files, filenames, or folder paths are ever uploaded to any cloud server.

3. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App integrates Firebase Crashlytics (including native NDK crash handling) to capture application crashes and system errors. Diagnostic reports include stack traces, device state at the time of crash, and high-level non-identifying build keys (e.g. build type, app version, subscription tier). Any error strings originating from conversion tasks are passed through a strict redaction filter before reporting to strip all personal file names, paths, and URIs.

4. Product Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand aggregate usage trends, such as conversion completion rates, screen views, and ad impression revenue. Analytics collection is subject to user consent signals (Consent Mode) and can be managed via in-app privacy settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob & Consent Management)

The free version of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID and device information in accordance with your consent preferences.

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App presents a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (User Messaging Platform / UMP) dialog before any ads are requested or loaded. You can change your consent preferences at any time by navigating to Settings → Privacy Options (Ad Consent).

Subscribers to Monthly Access or Lifetime Pro enjoy an ad-free experience with ad requests and ad-related measurement disabled.
